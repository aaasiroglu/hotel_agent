## About This Project

This AI assistant helps users find the most suitable hotel rooms based on their preferences.  
It leverages advanced search methods, image analysis, and weather integration to provide a seamless hotel booking experience.

### Key Features

- **Smart Room Search:**  
  Search for hotel rooms by describing your needs in natural language (e.g., "double room with sea view", "room with jacuzzi", "family room for 4 people").
- **Image Analysis:**  
  The assistant analyzes room images to match your preferences visually.
- **Live Weather Information:**  
  Get up-to-date weather details for the city of your selected hotel.
- **User-Friendly Interface:**  
  Start with example searches or type your own request.

### How to Use

1. **Start a Search:**  
   Click one of the example search buttons above or type your own hotel room request.
2. **Review Results:**  
   View detailed room descriptions, key attributes, images, and weather information.
3. **Refine Your Search:**  
   You can ask for specific features (e.g., "balcony", "air conditioning", "city view") or combine multiple criteria.

### Notes

- Please focus your queries on hotel room features and amenities.
- The assistant supports both English and Turkish queries.
- All results are generated using AI and may not reflect real-time hotel availability.
